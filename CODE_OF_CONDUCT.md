# Code of conduct

channelstack follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in its issues, pull requests, discussions and every other space the project runs.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and make room for people of every
background and level of experience. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination aren't
tolerated.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, write to the maintainer,
[Christian Bromann](https://github.com/christian-bromann), privately. Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer decides what happens, from a warning to a ban from the project, as
the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
